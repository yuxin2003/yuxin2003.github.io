Hello, world!

I created this website as a place to share some of my work, ideas, and things I find interesting along the way.

Most of what I write here will probably be related to research and teaching in communication science, AI, and health. From time to time, I may also use this space to reflect on projects I am working on, questions I am thinking about, or things I have learned from research and teaching.

I also hope to share some of my photography here. Photography has always been another way for me to observe places, people, and everyday life, so some posts may simply be visual notes from places I have lived in or visited.

This is still a work in progress, but I’m glad to have a small space of my own on the web.
